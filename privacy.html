Privacy Policy: yt-autopost

Last updated: 22 November 2026

yt-autopost is a private tool used by Anand Padmanabhan to upload videos to his own YouTube channels, including Curious Kids Science. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

The tool asks Google for permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube account (scope: youtube.upload). It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data, viewer data, comments or personal information.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload videos the owner has created. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

yt-autopost uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
